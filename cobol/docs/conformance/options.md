| 11.9.11 INTERMEDIATE ROUNDING GR 1 | with NATIVE arithmetic the intermediates' rounding is the implementor's | **implemented** under -std=2014 (queue item 22, 2026-10-07) as a **ruling**: TRUNCATION is the rule in force, and the clause's NEAREST-AWAY-FROM-ZERO, NEAREST-EVEN and PROHIBITED are applied wherever the stacks shed digits an intermediate cannot keep -- a quotient's last digit (the narrow stack's nine or more decimals, the wide stack's 38), the fraction digits a product sheds for room, the wide stack's alignment and fitting of 38 digits; PROHIBITED makes an inexact intermediate a size error (EC-SIZE-TRUNCATION, the statement's size error condition) and leaves exact ones alone.
